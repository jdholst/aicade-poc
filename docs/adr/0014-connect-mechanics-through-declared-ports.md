# Connect mechanics through declared ports

Mechanic Modules and Generated Mechanic Extensions will interact through typed input and output Mechanic Ports declared in their contracts and connected explicitly by the Final Game Spec. Sparkline validates payload schemas and connection compatibility and mediates delivery; mechanics may not import, discover, or call one another or publish unrestricted global events, allowing generated behavior to compose with trusted health, score, objective, and other mechanics without absorbing their responsibilities.
